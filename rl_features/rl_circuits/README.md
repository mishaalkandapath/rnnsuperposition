# rl_circuits: index

Results of the two-step-task circuit analysis (`docs/rl_circuits.md`). Model and transcoders as in
`circuit/rl_task/contrast_flow.py` (`RNN`, `TCS`); held-out windows = odd-episode trace windows of
`rl_trace_sequences/`; closed-loop episodes are rolled out afresh (seeded). All files are current; each `run_*.log`
is the log of the run that wrote the file of the same name.

| File | Made by | What it holds | Doc § |
|---|---|---|---|
| `contrast_flow.json` | `contrast_flow.py` | Exact contrastive attribution graphs, target the next choice (logit a1 − a2). Three contrasts on the core trial (state, action, reward swapped), 300 windows per core-trial pattern (choice × state × reward): mean target change, choice flips, flow by source and step, top features per step (all three gates), top edges | 2, 3 |
| `contrast_flow_value.json` | `contrast_flow.py --target value` | The same with the value as target | 3 |
| `gate_flow_shares.json` | `gate_flow_shares.py` | Per step (core D2, next D1, next GO) and gate, the next-choice flow through the gate's features in the exact contrastive graphs (state, action, reward contrasts; 800 held-out windows, 100 per core-trial pattern): signed sum, absolute sum and share of absolute flow | 3 |
| `switch_lag.json` | `switch_lag.py` | Trials from each settled reversal to the switch (first trial choosing the new action twice running) for the agent (sampled and preferred action) and the ideal observer on the agent's own outcomes, and the paired lag per reversal (shares later / same / earlier, counts), overall and split by direction (switching away from S1 or from S2) | 4, 7 |
| `evidence_patch.json` | `evidence_patch.py` | S1 vs S2 evidence (one trial's reward swapped, target the choice two trials later): the features carrying both (and each group's top features) patched in the real GRU at the next D2, the next D1 and the next GO; necessity and sufficiency per group vs random features | 7 |
| `flow_cosine_null.json` | `flow_cosine_null.py` | Per step, the cosine of the S1 and S2 evidence flow patterns with bootstrap intervals, a feature-shuffle null, a split-half ceiling and a label-shuffle null | 7 |
| `test1_flow.json` | `test1_flow.py` | Test 1 in the graph: by the earlier outcome's state, the next trial's state and agreement, the earlier evidence's flow through the features reading the next outcome, and which features | 7 |
| `state_channel.json` | `state_channel.py` | The rejected idea that S2 evidence rides the memory's "S2 reached" direction: alignment, persistence and a clamp of that direction (vs belief decoder, random) | 7 |
| `storage_asymmetry.json` | `storage_asymmetry.py` | Immediate and later effects and survival of S1 vs S2 evidence for the agent, the ideal observer and the fitted Bayesian; how much of the later effect the choice read-out component carries between choices (restoration) | 7 |
| `storage_cause.json` | `storage_cause.py` | Removal of the read-out component between choices (share of the later effect left), the read-out component step by step, event-level correlations, and the immediate effect by the agent's confidence | 7 |
| `value_check.json` | `value_check.py` | Is the value right beyond time: at the next D1 after a trial, rewarded minus unrewarded, the value head's change vs the actual discounted return's change (both minus their mean at that step), for trials reaching the believed-good and the believed-bad state | 6 |
| `credit_factorial.json` | `credit_factorial.py` | 600 windows, the core trial rerun in all 8 choice × state × reward cells: next choice per cell, reward effect per (choice, state), credit to the state / to the action, repeat bias, three-way term; the graphs of the two interactions seen from the reward, state and action sides; `held_in_h`: the state and action effects on h after D2, the next D1 and GO, split into each write's share | 2, 3 |
| `reversal_flow.json` | `reversal_flow.py` | 3000 closed-loop episodes: P(new good action) and the choice logit by trials since a reversal (agent vs ideal observer), the stay table, agreement with the observer; 600 switches: their timing, and the exact flow of the switch from each of the 5 previous outcomes (`old_world`: all replaced by the old belief's expectation; `last`: only the last one) | 1, 4 |
| `circuit_checks.json` | `circuit_checks.py` | Minimal circuits (real GRU): sufficiency and necessity, free and clamped, of each window's top-k features per step, k = 1–20, against random changed features; state swap, reward swap (800 windows each), reversal switch (400); share of patched gate values clamped | 5 |
| `value_circuit.json` | `value_circuit.py` | 3000 closed-loop episodes: V vs the return-to-go (γ 0.97) by trial, TD errors, V beyond time (belief, expected reward, previous reward, around reversals, at D2 by state reached, the inferred-value test at the reveal); the trial counter in h and the clock features; the late-vs-early time contrast (flow into the value and the choice, top features, patching); overlap with the credit features; behaviour by episode stage | 6 |
| `bayes_vs_mb.json` | `bayes_vs_mb.py` | Phase portraits (Ji-An et al. 2025): the agent's and the ideal observer's (binned, 6000 sampled points), the Bayesian symmetry; what separates the bands; Bayesian and two-value MB-RL models fitted to the agent's choices (held-out log-likelihood, ceiling); belief and MB-RL values decoded from h; the logit at GO split into carried memory and GO write (retention into the next trial, band variance explained, GO features by previous outcome); single-feature pushes and their persistence | 7 |
| `band_flow.json` | `band_flow.py` | The bands traced by attribution graphs: closed-loop windows with trial t−1's reward / state or trial t−2's reward swapped; exact flows into L(t), L(t+1) and the band offset (each run's distance from its input type's curve, original minus edited; oriented), the share kept one trial later (agent vs ideal observer), top offset / lost features, patching the top-k offset features per step vs random; `matched_logit`: at the same logit and input type, by the previous trial, the next change, the belief read from h and the ideal observer's, and the next-D1 detectors | 7 |
| `belief_axis.json` | `belief_axis.py` | One belief or two values: one trial's reward swapped in closed-loop windows; how much of its effect on the choice survives the next trial, split by same / other state, transition and agreeing / disagreeing (agent, ideal observer, fitted Bayesian, fitted two-value RL); exact graphs to the choice two trials later for S1 vs S2 evidence (flow-pattern cosine per step, top features), and the change in h along the belief decoder and in the rest | 7 |
| `rare_and_trace.json` | `rare_and_trace.py` | (1) Why an outcome seemed to last longer after a rare transition: the swap's effect right after and two trials on, by the next trial's transition (and agreeing / disagreeing), the effect of the next trial's own outcome, graph flows by step and the features that differ between rare and common cases. (2) Does the reward trace carry the bands: the trace subspace (top directions of the change in h, belief direction and read-out projected out) removed after the current choice (also all directions but the belief and read-out, and those two alone), share of the exact band offset removed vs random subspaces, plainly and preference-protected (only the part the choice logit and belief read-out cannot explain removed), and the exact vs straight-line offset | 7 |
| `trace_role.json` | `trace_role.py` | What the "was I just rewarded" trace is for: closed-loop play with the trace (or random 3-d subspaces) removed after every GO, plainly (set to its mean) and cleanly (`conditional`: set to what the belief predicts) (reward, agreement with the ideal observer, switching after reversals, stay table, bands, value vs return); the share of the band contrast's value difference removed with the trace; how often the bands change the preferred action; `belief_update` (the agent's belief update vs the ideal observer's); `belief_vs_choice_after_reversal`; `inertia` (the choice read-out direction, belief kept, removed before each GO) | 7 |
| `choice_lag.json` | `choice_lag.py` | Where the choice's lag behind the agent's own belief comes from: what predicts the logit beyond the belief (previous choice, its run length, x previous reward, time); matched pairs of "stick" vs "follow the belief" trials run on identical inputs from their own carried states, exact flow into the logit (by source, step, top features), the top D1 features' activations in both, pair differences and common histories; `split`: the difference split into belief directions, the choice read-out direction and the rest (exact graph flow of each part, and a real-GRU swap of each part just before the choice); patching the top-k features per step vs random | 7 |
| `belief_features.json` | `belief_features.py` | Which features hold and update the belief: per-step read-outs of the ideal observer's belief (R²); features most correlated with the belief per step, and beyond the trial's choice, state and previous outcome (partial correlation); a one-step split of the belief read-out after D1 (centred); the reward contrast's exact flow into the belief read-out after the next D1 (top features) | 7 |
| `belief_swap.json` | `belief_swap.py` | Does the agent act on the belief information: recipient / donor memory states (same two previous trials, ideal beliefs ≥ 2 apart); one part of the donor's memory (belief subspace, choice read-out direction, random subspaces, whole state) given to the recipient just before D1 or GO; change of the choice at this and the next two GOs (and as a share of the whole-state swap), and of the belief read-out | 7 |
| `rpe_analysis.json` | `rpe_analysis.py` | The agent's RPEs (TD errors of its value output) at state reveal and reward, correlated with Bayesian (Wang et al.'s "model-based"), Daw-style model-based RL, model-free SARSA(0) and reward-only references (learning rates chosen to fit best); Wang et al.'s residual test; a multiple regression | 7 |
| `inferred_value_flow.json` | `inferred_value_flow.py` | The inferred-value effect's circuit: trial t's outcome flips the belief so the other state is believed good, trial t+1 reaches it; contrast with trial t's reward swapped; exact flow into the value change at the reveal and into the value after it (by step, top features), patching the top-k features per step vs random | 6 |
| `neuro_analogues.json` | `neuro_analogues.py` | Mechanistic checks behind the systems-neuroscience parallels: the D1 detectors' preactivation split into input and memory parts (and the memory part with the reset gate of the other reward), how often each alone or both cross the threshold; how long the state reached stays readable (rare-transition trials); cross-step generalisation of read-outs of the belief information, the action preference and the previous reward, and top-feature overlap; the pull towards the previous choice and the miss readers by run length, after a miss and after a reward | 8 |
| `credit_steering.json` | `credit_steering.py` | The next-D1 (and D2 / GO) detectors of each (state, reward) cell, and steering them by label with the input unchanged: state, reward and double swaps; off only, on only, both; random-feature control; the real input swap for reference | 5 |
